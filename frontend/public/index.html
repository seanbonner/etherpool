EtherPool

One ETH address per payment pool. If the total due is reached by the due date, the recipient gets paid; otherwise senders can claim their ETH back.

Flow

  1. Create a pool with a total due, due date, and EOA recipient.
  2. Send ETH directly to the pool address.
  3. Call complete when funded.
  4. Claim failed-pool contributions or excess payments from the sending address.

Important

Do not send from exchanges or custodial wallets. Claims are keyed to the sending address. V1 recipients must be EOAs, not contracts, Safes, multisigs, or smart wallets. EtherPool has no admin controls, dev fee, or sweep.
